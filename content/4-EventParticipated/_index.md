During my internship, I participated in two events. Each one was a memorable experience that provided new, interesting, and useful knowledge, along with gifts and wonderful moments.


### [Event 1](4.1-Event1/)

 &emsp;**Event Name:** Saturday Meet Up

 &emsp;**Date and Time:** 09:00, June 6, 2025

 &emsp;**Location:** 26th Floor, Bitexco Financial Tower, 2 Hai Trieu Street, Sai Gon Ward, Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam

 &emsp;**Role:** Attendee

 &emsp;**Main Activities:** Attended an inspirational keynote delivered by Mr. Savik Dimitri on future career opportunities for software engineers in the ASEAN region. The event was followed by six presentations covering **Application Development**, **Machine Learning**, **Cloud Computing**, **Soft Skills**, **Big Data**, and **Career Development**.

 &emsp;**Key Takeaways:** Gained exposure to a variety of emerging technologies and applications, providing valuable insights for selecting suitable AWS services when designing future workshops.


### [Event 2](4.2-Event2/)

 &emsp;**Event Name:** FCAJ x Agentic AI Build Week: Show Up. Build. Pitch. WIN!

 &emsp;**Date and Time:** 09:00, July 25, 2025

 &emsp;**Location:** 26th Floor, Bitexco Financial Tower, 2 Hai Trieu Street, Sai Gon Ward, Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam

 &emsp;**Role:** Attendee

 &emsp;**Main Activities:** Attended an inspirational keynote on innovation and creative thinking, followed by four presentations delivered by the winning teams of the **FCAJ x Agentic AI Build Week** competition, where they shared their experiences in product development and hackathon participation.

 &emsp;**Key Takeaways:** Learned practical strategies for effective time management and teamwork when developing products under tight deadlines, including identifying each team member's strengths, rapidly analyzing requirements, assigning tasks efficiently, and optimizing operational costs.
