### Overall Evaluation


**1. Working Environment**

After experiencing 5 full working days at the office, I found the working environment to be professional, comfortable, and well organized. The workspace was clean, well equipped, and provided a productive environment for everyone to focus on their tasks. The desks were spacious enough, and the office was bright and pleasant. My favorite seat was the one near the window, as being able to look outside during short breaks helped me relax and regain focus after long periods of concentrated work.

If there is one aspect that could be improved, it would be the placement of power outlets at some workstations. Since the power cables currently need to be extended across the desk, they can occasionally be inconvenient to use. If the outlets could be integrated inside or underneath the desks, the workspace would become both more convenient and aesthetically pleasing.


**2. Support from Mentors and the Management Team**

The aspect that impressed me the most throughout the internship was the dedication of the mentors. Whenever I encountered difficulties, the mentors always took the time to provide guidance and explain the root cause of the problem instead of simply giving me the solution. They also encouraged me to ask questions, discuss ideas, and explore different approaches to solving problems. As a result, I became much more confident in both learning new technologies and applying them in practice.


**3. Relevance to My Academic Major**

The internship was closely aligned with my field of study while also providing practical knowledge that is not covered in depth in the university curriculum. Through the workshop and the product development process, I had the opportunity to learn about AWS Serverless architecture and understand how a complete system can be built by integrating multiple AWS services instead of managing the entire infrastructure manually using traditional approaches.

In addition to technical knowledge, I was also introduced to technical documentation using Hugo and GitHub Pages. This was a completely new experience for me and helped me realize that writing clear and well-structured documentation is just as important as writing high-quality source code for a software engineer.


**4. Learning Opportunities and Skill Development**

After 6 weeks of internship, what I gained was not limited to AWS knowledge but also included many essential skills required in a professional working environment. The internship helped me gain a deeper understanding of Serverless system design, Infrastructure as Code using AWS SAM, and the roles of services such as AWS Lambda, Amazon API Gateway, Amazon DynamoDB, Amazon Cognito, and AWS Secrets Manager within a complete Serverless application.


**5. Company Culture and Teamwork**

Throughout the internship, I experienced an open, positive, and learning-oriented working environment. Everyone was willing to support one another, communicate openly, and treat each other with respect. Although each mentor had their own responsibilities, they always made time to guide interns and share their practical experience.


**6. Internship Policy and Program**

Although the internship program did not provide financial support, I appreciated its flexible working arrangement. Requiring interns to work full-time at the office for only one day per week allowed me to balance my academic schedule, self-study time, and workshop activities effectively.



### Additional Feedback

**1. What I Appreciated Most**

The aspect I appreciated most throughout FCAJ 2026 was the dedication of the mentors. They not only provided technical guidance but also encouraged interns to explore new areas, take on different responsibilities, and develop their own solutions instead of simply following instructions. This approach significantly enhanced my learning experience and helped me become a more independent learner.


**2. Areas for Improvement**

From my perspective, the internship program was very well organized. However, I would like to offer a few suggestions that could further improve the experience for future interns:

***First, regarding the office workspace.*** Because the desks are arranged relatively close to one another, the workspace can occasionally feel crowded, especially when many people are working in the office at the same time. If space permits, increasing the distance between desks would create a more comfortable working environment and help improve concentration.

***Second, regarding the office registration process.*** Currently, office attendance approvals are usually confirmed quite close to the scheduled working day. This was not a significant issue for me because I was on summer break and living relatively close to the office. However, for interns who need to coordinate their university schedules or travel from farther away, receiving approval approximately one day earlier—or closing the registration period one day earlier—would make personal planning much more convenient.


**3. Overall Impression**

After 6 weeks of working in a real-world environment, I believe FCAJ is a well-structured internship program, especially for students who have limited experience in software development.

What I value most is that the program not only teaches participants how to use AWS technologies but also helps them understand system design principles, software development processes, and professional working practices within an enterprise environment. These are valuable practical experiences that are difficult to obtain solely through university courses.

For me personally, FCAJ has been a meaningful first step in preparing for the transition from university life to a professional software engineering career.



### Suggestions and Expectations

After completing the program, I felt that six weeks of internship together with approximately two weeks for completing the final report was sufficient to become familiar with the product development process. However, this period was still relatively short for gaining an in-depth understanding of the entire system and the technologies involved. Most of the knowledge I acquired came from self-directed learning combined with the valuable guidance provided by the mentors.

If possible in the future, I hope FCAJ could collaborate with universities to establish an **"Enterprise Semester"** program, where students would have the opportunity to work within a company for an entire academic semester (approximately 4 - 5 months). A longer internship period would allow students to participate in more stages of a real-world software project, gain a deeper understanding of software development processes, and accumulate more practical experience.

Finally, I believe FCAJ is a well-designed training program for students and individuals who have little or no professional software development experience. The program not only introduces participants to modern cloud technologies but also helps develop technical thinking, self-learning abilities, and professional working skills. If I have the opportunity in the future, I would be delighted to continue participating in FCAJ after graduation to further expand my knowledge and gain additional practical experience.
