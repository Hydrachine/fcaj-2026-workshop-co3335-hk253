**On this page**, you will need to introduce your worklog. **How** did you complete it? How many weeks did you take to complete the program? **What** did you do in those weeks?

Typically, and as a standard, a worklog is carried out over about 3 months (throughout the internship period) with weekly contents as follows:

**Week 1:** [Get acquainted with FCAJ 2026 participants, form a project team, and explore fundamental AWS services](1.1-week1/)

**Week 2:** [Explore Amazon VPC, Amazon EC2, and the initial project deployment architecture](1.2-week2/)

**Week 3:** [Explore Amazon S3, MobaXterm, and evaluate two prototype deployment approaches](1.3-week3/)

**Week 4:** [Team task assignment and introduction to Amazon DynamoDB](1.4-week4/)

**Week 5:** [Explore Amazon Cognito and Amazon CloudWatch, and design the project frontend](1.5-week5/)

**Week 6:** [Collaborate on frontend development and API integration](1.6-week6/)

**Week 7:** [Resolve project issues and prepare the individual report using the Hugo template](1.7-week7/)

**Week 8:** [Share knowledge with the "AWS Study Group VN" community and finalize the internship report](1.8-week8/)
