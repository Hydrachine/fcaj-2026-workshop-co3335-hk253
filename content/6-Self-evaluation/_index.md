During my internship at **Amazon Web Services Viet Nam Company Limited** as a participant in the **Workforce Bootcamp - First Cloud AI Journey 2026** program from **June 15, 2026** to **August 15, 2026**, I had the opportunity to learn, develop my skills, and apply the knowledge I had acquired at university in a professional working environment.

Throughout the internship, I contributed to **API design for the BK-Sync team project** and was responsible for **managing the team's posts in the ["AWS Study Group VN"](https://www.facebook.com/groups/awsstudygroupfcj) Facebook community**. Through these responsibilities, I **strengthened my understanding of AWS Serverless system design**, **backend API design for frontend integration**, **technical documentation** and **project documentation management**, while also **improving my teamwork**, **technical communication**, and **collaboration skills throughout the software development process**.

In addition to technical knowledge, I consistently strived to complete assigned tasks on time, complied with the internship program's regulations, and proactively communicated with both mentors and teammates whenever I encountered challenges or had suggestions for improving the team's work.
